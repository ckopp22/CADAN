How to Play

Rolling: Each turn, roll 6 dice. Keep any you like and re-roll the rest, up to 3 rolls in total.

Building: Spend your dice on roads, settlements, cities and knights on your own island. Each type must be built in ascending point order.

Knight jokers: Each knight you build lets you use its resource as a wildcard once per game.

Gold: Trade 2 gold for any 1 resource.

Winning: Build nothing on a turn and you mark an X (-2 points). After 15 turns each, the highest score wins.
